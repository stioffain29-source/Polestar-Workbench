---
name: Ports and Logistics report scope
description: What the fortnightly Ports and Logistics Intelligence generator may and may not claim now that its exports are customer-facing documents.
---

The Ports and Logistics Intelligence generator lives inside the Workbench and its
Word and PDF exports are customer-facing. Nothing internal belongs in them: no
working notes, no reviewer or effort logging, no notice that the work is a trial,
and no editor warning text.

A short report is the correct outcome when the collected material is thin. Never
pad towards the configured item target, never invent a corroborating source, an
event date, a publication date or an operating consequence, and never present a
coverage target as a confirmed customer asset.

**Why:** the feeds behind this report were built for other topics, so a fortnight
over APAC and Oceania regularly yields two or three genuinely material
developments. Earlier framing made that shortfall look like a defect and invited
padding; the shortfall is a collection fact to report plainly.

**How to apply:** when the item count looks low, check collection and
classification before touching generation, and state the shortfall in the summary
to the owner rather than in the customer document. Keep the asset roster labelled
provisional until the owner confirms the real footprint. Publishing or
distributing a report still needs explicit instruction.
