---
name: Shipping Watch monthly product
description: Defines the durable cadence, analytical focus and output structure for Shipping Watch.
---

Shipping Watch is a monthly product built around month-on-month change, trends,
operational consequences and a next-30-day outlook. It is not a weekly incident
digest.

**Why:** The owner judged the weekly, event-led structure unfit for purpose.
Clients need to understand what changed, what trend is forming, what it means
operationally and what to watch next.

**How to apply:** Use current calendar month, previous month and three-month
comparisons only when supported by historical data. Keep regional and thematic
assessments separate, omit empty metrics and inactive locations, and place
material deduplicated incident detail at the back. Preserve all material rows;
the register may flow beyond its starting page rather than truncating data.
