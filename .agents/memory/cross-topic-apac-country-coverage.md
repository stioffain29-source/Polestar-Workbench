---
name: Cross-topic APAC country coverage
description: Rules for keeping Australia, China, Vietnam, Cambodia and Hong Kong represented across live monitor intake.
---

Treat country coverage as a shared registry-and-feed contract, not a report-layer adjustment. Australia, mainland China, Vietnam, Cambodia and Hong Kong must remain attributable and have dedicated topic feeds wherever country-news collection drives a monitor. Hong Kong is an independent reporting geography rather than a China alias.

**Why:** Sparse reporting across these markets came from inconsistent country lists and missing targeted feeds, not from severity logic. Broad regional queries are rank-capped and cannot reliably substitute for country feeds.

**How to apply:** When adding or changing a live topic, assert target-country feed presence and canonical aliases together. Keep centroids aligned with aliases. Improve collection and attribution rather than loosening relevance gates.
