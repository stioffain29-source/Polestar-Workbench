---
name: Conflict canonical current set
description: Rules separating current Conflict incidents from context and binding every client-facing section to one canonical set.
---

Conflict relevance is broader than a current operational incident. Historical or cumulative casualty totals, official statements, political rhetoric, policy positions, threat reporting, surrender/demobilisation and peace-process developments remain available as context but cannot drive incident totals, severity, country/type counts, latest incident, activity areas or posture. A strategic/context record becomes current only when it reports a discrete same-event kinetic occurrence.

**Why:** conflict keywords and background snippets caused political “attack” rhetoric and multi-year death aggregates to become Extreme weekly incidents. Relevance alone cannot answer what real-world event the article is reporting.

**How to apply:** classify title-led event semantics before dedupe and metrics. Political verbal-attack homonyms and aggregate reporting win before broad kinetic tokens; preserve genuine official reporting of a specific attack through a concrete same-event override.

One final distinct, validated, current-window incident set is authoritative for Fast Facts, BLUF, Top Activity Areas, Other Watched Theatres, What Matters, Watch Next and Polestar View. Same-event and running-operation collapse happens before all calculations. Context is stored separately.

**Why:** filtering or deduplicating after individual sections are built produces internally contradictory counts, severity and narrative. A section-specific fallback silently recreates raw-row inflation.

**How to apply:** expose accepted incident IDs plus a stable canonical fingerprint, and derive every client-facing section from those rows. Watch Next items retain accepted supporting IDs; generated Conflict prose with a mismatched basis is dropped, while analyst edits remain distinct.

Conflict Watch client order is Cover, Fast Facts, BLUF, Top Activity Areas, Other Watched Theatres, What Matters for Business, Watch Next, Polestar View, Disclaimer. Related Incidents remains available in Workbench but is not rendered in the client PDF. Country/subsection headings must stay with body text and Polestar View stays together.

**Why:** the client product is an analytical brief, not an incident register, and direct PDF pagination previously orphaned theatre headings.

**How to apply:** keep preview and direct PDF order in lockstep; use measured keep-with-next blocks and flexible page breaks rather than fixed physical page numbers.
