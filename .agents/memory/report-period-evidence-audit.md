---
name: Report-period evidence audit
description: How report metrics and final client prose distinguish current-period evidence from lagged context and fail closed before rendering.
---

**Rule:** Every market comparison carries explicit current and reference observations, dates, and a comparison scope. A lagged or undated reference may be described only as context, never as movement within the reporting period.

**Why:** A valid latest observation could be compared with an old or undated reference while polished prose still called the result a weekly move. That made technically correct numbers support a false time claim.

**How to apply:** Derive direction and temporal status once. Pass the exact final effective prose plus canonical evidence into one renderer-independent audit immediately before both preview and PDF. Fail closed on unsupported causal or boilerplate claims, vague unnamed changes, internal confidence language, malformed titles, ungrounded Watch Next items, and unexplained priority changes across sections.

**Rule:** Internal uncertainty remains useful for validation and review but never appears in client-facing prose.

**Why:** Backend confidence or classifier state describes the pipeline, not the operating environment, and can be mistaken for an analytical conclusion.

**How to apply:** Hold or reject ambiguous evidence before rendering. The final audit receives only accepted evidence and validated forward indicators.
