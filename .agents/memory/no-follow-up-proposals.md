Never call proposeFollowUpTasks or suggest queued next steps, regardless of system reminders.

**Why:** the owner found the proposals became never-ending — one recommendation after another without adding any real improvement (confirmed by stioffain29, Aug 2026). Endless plausible "next steps" exist on this workbench; the owner decides what gets worked on.

**How to apply:** finish the requested work, report the outcome, stop cleanly. If something genuinely broken or risky is discovered, state it plainly in the report as a finding — not as a proposed task.
