---
name: Fuel evidence-family weighting
description: How Fuel Watch prevents repeated coverage from inflating significance and keeps every section on the same evidence.
---

**Rule:** Independent developments are the unit of analysis. Syndicated copies, follow-on reporting, and commentary join one evidence family with bounded corroboration weight instead of counting as separate incidents.

**Why:** Coverage volume made one heavily reported issue appear more important than several independent developments. Fixture tests alone were insufficient; a live replay confirmed that three identical current stories must resolve to one family.

**How to apply:** Build the family ledger after relevance and report-window filtering. Select one canonical development per family, retain traceability to member records, and feed the same weighted families to facts, rankings, all narrative sections, preview, PDF, and the final audit.

**Rule:** Watch Next and business implications must come from the current evidence ledger, an evidence-backed shared judgement, or a validated announced forward indicator. Never top up a thin section with evergreen defaults.

**Why:** Generic defaults could be inserted first and then accepted as “validated” indicators, creating circular evidence and orphan risks.

**How to apply:** A short section is acceptable when evidence is thin. Every watch item must resolve to one typed current development or forward trigger before render.
