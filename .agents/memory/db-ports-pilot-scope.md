---
name: DB Ports pilot boundary
description: Why the DB Ports work remains a limited internal trial rather than a launched client intelligence service.
---

Treat DB Ports as an internal feasibility pilot within the existing Workbench, not a separate product or an approved client service. Permission to proceed with the pilot was not permission to publish, distribute, enable continuous collection, or change production data.

**Why:** The pilot is intended to establish whether source coverage, verification quality and real analyst effort support a sustainable fortnightly service. The client asset list was not supplied. The initially researched official notices did not justify an approved, filled-out edition; presenting a sparse working draft is preferable to padding it or claiming comprehensive coverage.

**How to apply:** Keep coverage targets explicitly provisional until the owner confirms the actual footprint. Research assistance may record what sources say and where checks failed, but must not impersonate analyst or independent-review attestations, infer client exposure, or invent human working hours. An internal approval is still not authorisation for external distribution. Seek explicit scope expansion before turning this trial into a scheduled or published service.
