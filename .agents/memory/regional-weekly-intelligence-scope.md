name: Regional weekly intelligence scope
description: Durable editorial and visual rules for APAC Weekly and Middle East Weekly.
---

APAC Weekly and Middle East Weekly are regional business-operating intelligence products, not security-only reports. Their evidence set spans security, political, regulatory, weather/natural hazards, cyber and operational disruption, but every included item must have credible operational relevance.

**Why:** The owner amended both products to provide a complete regional operating picture while retaining the existing branding, report structure and export workflow.

**How to apply:** Cluster source records by underlying event before selecting approximately five to eight developments. Prefer category and country diversity without filling quotas or inventing content. Preserve corroborating evidence. Generated outlooks must assess next-week operating conditions across all material categories rather than use a quiet-security fallback. Add visuals only when real underlying data answers location, risk-driver, direction, timing or exposure questions, and keep preview/PDF parity.
