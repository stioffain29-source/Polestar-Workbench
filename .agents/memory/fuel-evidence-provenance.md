---
name: Fuel evidence provenance
description: Durable rules for Fuel cross-read admission, evidence-family geography, generated prose support, and stale cache precedence.
---

Fuel-native developments and cross-topic evidence have different admission boundaries. Native Fuel records may include directly material producer, pricing, regulatory, aviation-cost, outlook, supply and potential-condition evidence. Shipping, Energy, Conflict and other cross-reads require an explicit demonstrated Fuel consequence; generic power, war, transport or maritime activity is not enough.

**Why:** applying the explicit-consequence rule to every path removed valid Fuel-native producer, outlook and aviation evidence. Applying it only at the cross-read boundary removes generic load-shedding and conflict leakage without shrinking legitimate Fuel coverage.

**How to apply:** keep one strict cross-read Fuel-consequence predicate at the merge boundary. Do not reuse it as a blanket filter over already-qualified Fuel-native records or specialist Fuel tables.

Evidence-family clustering must be transitive. If a later strongly matching rewrite bridges two partial groups, merge both groups. For global/regional market families, reconcile geography across the family: publisher or ingest-country stamps do not become affected countries, while concrete site events retain their physical geography.

**Why:** first-match-only grouping left one global ship-fuel development split when earlier coverage had already seeded two partial groups. Isolated three-row tests passed while the full production dataset still failed.

**How to apply:** test production-shaped families in the presence of earlier related rows, not only as isolated fixtures. Keep semantic/time thresholds conservative, but union every group a bridging record strongly matches.

Fuel analytical objects must retain supporting incident and evidence-family IDs through What Happened and Watch Next. Generated items with no current support are excluded before rendering. Stale generated/cache prose never outranks the current canonical fingerprint; genuine analyst edits are retained separately and flagged when their basis changes.

**Why:** plain strings erased provenance and let validators discover unsupported text too late. The generator, renderer and publication audit must use the same evidence binding.

**How to apply:** keep machine-readable provenance beside rendered text, verify the bound text against current IDs, and treat Potential incidents as valid Watch Next support without allowing them into current-condition sections.
