---
name: Canonical report risk
description: Durable authority and precedence rules for scheduled-report risk ratings.
---

Scheduled topic-report risk must be derived from the same final, analyst-curated evidence set that drives the report’s Fast Facts and client output. Narrative tier words are never a risk input, and a saved rating cannot override a computable canonical rating.

**Why:** independent raw-row, prose-scanning and saved-override paths allowed one report to show a canonical severity peak while cards or old narrative declared a different risk level.

**How to apply:** apply exclusions and severity corrections before risk calculation. Use each specialist’s authority: classified/deduplicated Conflict incidents, clustered Flashpoint incidents, Fuel’s full continuity-aware canonical facts, and Shipping’s maritime board. Use generic filtered-window evidence only for topics without a specialist model.
