Documented limitation
------------------------------------------------------------
jsPDF auto-registers the 14 PDF standard fonts (Helvetica,
Times, Courier, Symbol, ZapfDingbats) in the PDF font
dictionary at constructor time. They are NOT embedded and
NOT selected by any content-stream Tf operator. Every text
draw in the codebase is routed through setRoboto(), which
calls pdf.setFont('Roboto', ...). Removing the standard
fonts from the dictionary is not exposed by jsPDF's public
API; the safe proof is the per-page Tf inventory above,
which shows /Roboto is the sole font ever activated.
