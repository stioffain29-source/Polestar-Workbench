CURRENT SITUATION
Security incidents in West Papua during the reporting period were concentrated in Papua, Yahukimo and
Jayapura, with the remainder unlocated. Insurgency and violent crime were the principal concerns.
Yahukimo and Papua generated repeat reporting within the period, so the pressure there is sustained rather
than a one-off. The incidents do not currently form a single nationwide pattern; risk varies by location, and
movement decisions should be based on current conditions at the destination.

CRIME, THEFT & ROBBERY

Crime, theft and violent incidents were reported, including violent crime, terrorism and theft, concentrated in
Papua Pegunungan, Papua Selatan and Papua Tengah. The most serious reported was House of
Representatives condemns shooting of civilians by OPM in Yahukimo (Papua Pegunungan), assessed as
High severity. Reporting of this activity eased against the previous week. This reporting is most relevant to
staff safety, premises, vehicles and the secure movement of cash and assets. Incidents of this kind
threaten staff, premises and the movement of cash and assets.
OTHER OPERATIONAL DISRUPTION

Operational disruption to transport, utilities or connectivity was reported, including other security and
aviation, concentrated in Papua Pegunungan and Papua. The most serious reported was West Papua
rebels kill three indigenous Papuans, assessed as High severity. Reporting of this activity eased against the
previous week. This reporting is most relevant to transport, power, communications and the operations that
depend on them. Disruption of this kind can interrupt operations, logistics and connectivity.
NATURAL HAZARDS

Natural-hazard and environmental disruption was reported, including natural hazard, concentrated in
Papua, Papua Pegunungan and Papua Tengah. The most serious reported was Magnitude 4.1 earthquake
shakes Sarmi, Papua, assessed as Low severity. Reporting of this activity picked up against the previous
week. This reporting is most relevant to transport links, utilities, site access and the safety of outdoor
operations. Hazards of this kind can interrupt transport, utilities and site access.

OPERATIONAL IMPACT
- The event is close to, or significant enough for, local movement and planning to be reviewed.

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
MOVEMENT

- Confirm current route conditions before travel into affected districts.
- Avoid non-essential after-hours movement where recent violence has occurred.
- Allow extra travel time where police activity or local gatherings are reported.
SITE SECURITY

- Review access arrangements at sites close to reported incidents.
- Confirm after-hours escalation procedures with guards and site managers.
- Check that emergency contacts and reporting lines remain current.

OUTLOOK: NEXT SEVEN DAYS
Insurgency is likely to remain the main concern during the next seven days. Yahukimo, Papua and Jayapura
saw repeat reporting within the period and are the most likely setting for renewed incidents. Reporting
volume increased compared with the previous period. The coming days should be judged against that
direction rather than any single event.
ESCALATION INDICATORS

- Larger or coordinated protest mobilisation, or labour action spreading to key sites and corridors
- Further casualty-bearing or higher-severity violence close to staffed premises
- Spread of incidents beyond Central Highlands (Highland & Central Papua) and Jayapura & North Coast
(Papua Province) into new districts

POLESTAR VIEW
This period the reporting that carried real weight was insurgency; the rest of the week's items were routine
by comparison. Weighed together, the week's reporting bears most on both movement planning and the
safety of staff and premises, and that is where checks will pay off first. On assessment, the risk is localised
and varies by location, and it is best managed through location-specific decisions rather than country-wide
measures. The key judgement is that insurgency drives the current concern, while other categories
contributed limited, separate disruption. Businesses should prioritise verified route and site information for
Papua and Yahukimo over broad precautionary measures.
