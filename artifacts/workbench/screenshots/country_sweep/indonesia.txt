CURRENT SITUATION
Security incidents in Indonesia during the reporting period were concentrated in Jakarta, Indonesia and
Padang. Natural hazard and other operational disruption were the principal concerns. Jakarta and Banten
generated repeat reporting within the period, so the pressure there is sustained rather than a one-off. The
incidents do not currently form a single nationwide pattern; risk varies by location, and movement decisions
should be based on current conditions at the destination.

POLESTAR VIEW
Weighed together, the week's reporting bears most on both movement planning and the safety of staff and
premises, and that is where checks will pay off first. On assessment, the risk is localised and varies by
location, and it is best managed through location-specific decisions rather than country-wide measures.
The key judgement is that natural hazard drives the current concern, while other categories contributed
limited, separate disruption. Businesses should prioritise verified route and site information for Jakarta and
Indonesia over broad precautionary measures.
