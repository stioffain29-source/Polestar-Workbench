CURRENT SITUATION
Security incidents in Thailand during the reporting period were concentrated in Nonthaburi, Chonburi and
Narathiwat, with the remainder unlocated. Violent crime and other operational disruption were the principal
concerns. Narathiwat and Nonthaburi generated repeat reporting within the period, so the pressure there is
sustained rather than a one-off. The incidents do not currently form a single nationwide pattern; risk varies
by location, and movement decisions should be based on current conditions at the destination.

POLESTAR VIEW
This period the reporting that carried real weight was other operational disruption around Narathiwat; the
rest of the week's items were routine by comparison. Weighed together, the week's reporting bears most on
both movement planning and the safety of staff and premises, and that is where checks will pay off first.
On assessment, the risk is localised and varies by location, and it is best managed through location-specific
decisions rather than country-wide measures. The key judgement is that violent crime drives the current
concern, while other categories contributed limited, separate disruption. Businesses should prioritise
verified route and site information for Nonthaburi and Chonburi over broad precautionary measures.
