# Southeast/East Asia source checks

Research run date: 2026-09-22  
Required publication window: 2026-09-09 through 2026-09-22 inclusive.

Six targeted searches were run across the assigned geographies, including Bahasa Indonesia/Melayu and Chinese queries. Only primary-source pages were treated as evidence. Search-result text was used for discovery, not confirmation.

## Singapore

- Checked Maritime and Port Authority of Singapore circulars, notices and port-information results.
- The relevant port-call data circular found was published 2026-09-02, outside the window. A 2026-09-17 notice concerned Baltic Sea navigation rather than assigned-area port operations.
- Gap: no in-window primary notice with demonstrated Singapore port, terminal, cargo, access or continuity implications was substantiated.

## Malaysia

- Checked Malaysia Marine Department port circulars and Notices to Mariners in Bahasa/English.
- Confirmed and retained NTM 176(T)/2026, issued 2026-09-14, covering PM3 CAA drilling and vessel movements beginning 2026-09-22.
- The port-circular index search result exposed an item dated 2026-09-15, but the fetched dynamic index returned no rows or document link. It remains unconfirmed and was not retained.

## Indonesia

- Checked BMKG maritime warnings and port forecasts using Bahasa queries.
- Port forecasts for Tanjung Emas, Semayang and Tanjung Kalian were routine forecasts and did not demonstrate operational disruption.
- Failed fetch: the promising BMKG eastern Central Java wave-warning download returned `Invalid or expired token`; its full contents, issue date and operational implications could not be verified. No Indonesia lead was retained.

## Philippines

- Checked Philippine Ports Authority, Philippine Coast Guard and port schedule/advisory results.
- Results were routine cooperation news, procurement documents, an old March readiness statement, or a vessel-schedule page without a demonstrated disruption.
- Gap: no qualifying in-window primary suspension, restriction, cargo/compliance action or continuity notice was substantiated.

## Mainland China

- Checked Maritime Safety Administration and General Administration of Customs sources using Chinese queries.
- The customs container-fitness announcement identified in the results was dated 2026-09-08, outside the window. The MSA operational notice surfaced by search covered April-June 2026.
- Gap: no in-window primary mainland China notice with demonstrated assigned materiality was confirmed.

## Hong Kong

- Checked Hong Kong Marine Department notices and the Electronic Business System.
- Fetched the primary eBS page and verified a scheduled outage of all services on 2026-09-22. It is retained as **hold** because the page does not show when the notice was published.
- A Marine Department restricted-area PDF was discovered but not retained because the search result did not establish its publication date or complete operative period.

## Taiwan

- Checked Taiwan port/marine results through English and Chinese query variants in the Greater China search.
- No Taiwan primary result meeting both the date window and operational-materiality test surfaced.
- Gap: no qualifying Taiwan lead was substantiated; no source page was available to fetch.

## Outcome

- **Candidate:** 1 Malaysia maritime-access notice.
- **Hold:** 1 Hong Kong eBS outage notice lacking a visible publication date.
- No event severity, realized delay, analyst approval or database publication is asserted.
