Arabian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, Red Sea
Threat Level

Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) assesses that the regional maritime security threat level across the Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman remains ELEVATED amid ongoing military activity and regional tensions affecting commercial shipping.

CMF regional threat assessments are issued in coordination with the Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) and provide industry-releasable guidance on risk management for merchant vessels transiting CMF operating areas.

Mariners should maintain heightened situational awareness, monitor VHF Channel 16, and consult UKMTO and JMIC products for the latest guidance.
