U.S. Forces Complete Another Round of Strikes Against Iran

July 8, 2026 Release Number 20260708-01 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

TAMPA, Fla. – U.S. Central Command (CENTCOM) forces completed an additional round of strikes to degrade Iran's ability to attack commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and to hold at risk Iranian naval and missile capabilities that threaten U.S. forces and partners in the region.

The operation targeted Iranian military infrastructure supporting anti-ship cruise missile and fast-attack craft operations near the Arabian Gulf and Gulf of Oman. CENTCOM assessed the strikes reduced immediate risk to merchant vessels transiting the Strait of Hormuz.

U.S. forces remain postured to defend freedom of navigation and to respond to threats against commercial shipping, U.S. Navy warships, and coalition partners operating in international waters.
