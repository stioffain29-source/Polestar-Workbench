JMIC products

    Accurate and timely information for the protection of mariners

    Joint Maritime Information Center (JMIC) is a Combined Maritime Forces (CMF) capability operating in close cooperation with maritime stakeholder organizations that support a Single Information Framework (SIF) process. The JMIC seeks, where possible, participation from multiple military stakeholders including Naval Cooperation and Guidance for Shipping (NCAGS) and Information Fusion Centers (IFC) specialists to fuse open source.
