UKMTO has received a report of an incident 25NM northeast of Oman.

A Container Ship was hit by an unknown projectile which caused damage to some containers. The vessel and crew are reported safe and the vessel is proceeding to its next port of call.

Authorities are investigating. Mariners are advised to exercise caution in the area and report suspicious activity to UKMTO.
