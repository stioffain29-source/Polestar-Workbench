Fix report date windows across all report builders.

This applies to all report types and all topics.

Do not change the database.
Do not delete records.
Do not re-import data.
Do not change dashboard/archive pages.

Rules:

1. Weekly reports

All Weekly Brief / Weekly Report outputs must use a maximum 10-day reporting window.

Default:
- last 7 days

Maximum allowed:
- 10 days

Do not include records older than 10 days in weekly reports.

This applies to:
- PNG country report
- Papua country report
- Fuel
- Fertiliser
- Civil Protests & Civil Unrest
- Energy / Grid
- Shipping
- Cargo Watch
- Flashpoint
- Missile Strike Tracker reports

2. Monthly reports

All Monthly Report outputs must use a maximum 35-day reporting window.

Default:
- last 30 days

Maximum allowed:
- 35 days

Do not include records older than 35 days in monthly reports.

3. Related incidents tables

For weekly reports:
- only show records inside the selected weekly window
- maximum 10 to 15 rows
- sorted newest first

For monthly reports:
- only show records inside the selected monthly window
- maximum 15 to 25 rows
- sorted newest first

4. Reporting period

Show the reporting period clearly near the top of every report.

Examples:
Weekly: Reporting period: 14 May 2026 - 23 May 2026
Monthly: Reporting period: 19 April 2026 - 23 May 2026

Date ranges must run oldest to newest.

5. Older records

Do not include older records in the report table.

Add a note where useful:
“Older records remain available in the Workbench.”

6. Do not affect

Do not apply these caps to:
- dashboard topic pages
- incident archive pages
- country archive views
- map views
- full database views

Acceptance criteria:
- Weekly reports never show more than 10 days of records.
- Monthly reports never show more than 35 days of records.
- Related incident tables obey the selected report window.
- Historical dumps disappear from weekly reports.
- Dashboard and archive views remain unchanged.
