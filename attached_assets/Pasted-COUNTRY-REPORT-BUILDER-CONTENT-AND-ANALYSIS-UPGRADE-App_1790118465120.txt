COUNTRY REPORT BUILDER — CONTENT AND ANALYSIS UPGRADE

Apply this to ALL Polestar Country Reports.

This is a GENERIC BUILDER change.

DO NOT hard-code:

countries
cities
provinces
industries
specific incidents
specific threats
specific companies

The selected country and reporting period must determine the content dynamically.

DO NOT modify the map in this task.

Map treatment will be handled separately.


==================================================
1. PURPOSE
==================================================

Country Reports are weekly COUNTRY INTELLIGENCE PRODUCTS.

They are NOT:

security incident summaries
crime reports
news digests
feed summaries
lists of whatever stories were collected

The report must answer:

WHAT CHANGED THIS WEEK?

WHAT MATTERS TO A BUSINESS OPERATING IN THIS COUNTRY?

WHERE IS THE EXPOSURE?

WHAT SHOULD THE CLIENT WATCH NEXT?

==================================================
4. BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT
==================================================

Keep:

BOTTOM LINE UP FRONT

Target:

100 to 130 words.

This is the executive summary.

It should identify:

the most important change this week
one or two secondary issues where material
the principal business consequence
whether conditions are worsening, improving or broadly stable

Do NOT mention:

validated event counts
source counts
reporting volume
feed volume
database records

Do NOT write:

“The most serious validated development…”

“Reporting volume remained…”

“1 validated event against 1…”

The client wants the judgement, not the mechanics behind it.


==================================================
5. CURRENT SITUATION
==================================================

Target:

250 to 400 words.

This should provide the COUNTRY OPERATING PICTURE.

Use analytical prose.

Do not structure it as:

Country:
Incident:
Country:
Incident:

Explain how the week's developments affect:

people
travel
operations
assets
logistics
supply chains
workforce
compliance
continuity

where relevant.

Connect developments where there is a genuine relationship.

Do not manufacture connections simply to fill space.


==================================================
6. KEY DEVELOPMENTS
==================================================

Where sufficient material exists, include:

KEY DEVELOPMENTS

Target:

3 to 6 developments.

Do NOT force six.

A quiet country may have fewer.

Each development must contain:

LOCATION

DATE

SHORT TITLE

CATEGORY

CURRENT SEVERITY

ASSESSMENT

What happened and what is the current operational consequence?

POLESTAR VIEW

Why does this matter to business?

7 DAY INDICATOR

What specific change should be watched next?

Do not use identical text across different developments.


==================================================
7. SEVERITY
==================================================

Use only:

INSIGNIFICANT
LOW
MODERATE
HIGH
EXTREME

Severity means CURRENT CONSEQUENCE.

It does NOT mean:

headline importance
number of sources
political importance
media attention
dramatic language

LOW

Limited local impact.

MODERATE

Credible disruption or threat requiring operational adjustment.

HIGH

Significant violence, disruption or restriction with material effects on people, assets, operations, travel or continuity.

EXTREME

Major or widespread impact, mass casualties, severe infrastructure loss or sustained large scale disruption.

Geographically local events may still be HIGH.

National policy changes may remain LOW or MODERATE where operational consequences are limited.


==================================================
8. BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS
==================================================

Create a proper:

BUSINESS IMPLICATIONS

section.

Target:

150 to 250 words.

This should synthesise what the week's intelligence means for businesses operating in the country.

Consider only where relevant:

People and Travel

Operations and Assets

Supply Chain and Logistics

Regulatory and Market Access

Energy and Utilities

Workforce

Business Continuity

Do not simply repeat the Key Developments.


==================================================
9. RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
==================================================

Recommended Actions must respond to THIS WEEK'S intelligence.

Do not automatically generate the same headings every week.

Use action areas only where required, such as:

MOVEMENT

TRAVEL

SITE SECURITY

LOGISTICS

SUPPLY CHAIN

WORKFORCE

REGULATORY

CYBER

BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Actions must be specific.

Do NOT generate generic boilerplate such as:

“check emergency contacts”

“brief staff”

“monitor the situation”

“confirm journey plans”

unless the evidence creates a specific reason to do so.


==================================================
10. 7 DAY WATCH
==================================================

Run a separate forward search for the NEXT SEVEN DAYS.

Search for:

planned protests
industrial action
elections
political deadlines
court decisions
regulatory changes
public holidays where operationally significant
major gatherings
security operations
transport closures
weather systems
flood warnings
fuel or power disruption
border changes
other scheduled events with business relevance

Target:

up to 5 useful items.

Each item:

DATE
LOCATION
EVENT

WHY IT MATTERS

WATCH

Do not fill this section with low value calendar events if better forward risks exist.


==================================================
11. ACTIONS AND OUTLOOK
==================================================

Keep the existing ACTIONS & OUTLOOK concept if required by the current design.

However, separate the functions clearly:

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS

What should the client do now?

OUTLOOK: NEXT SEVEN DAYS

What is likely to matter next?

Do not mix current actions and forward assessment into the same paragraph.


==================================================
12. POLESTAR VIEW
==================================================

Keep:

POLESTAR VIEW

Target:

100 to 140 words.

This is the final analytical judgement.

It should explain:

what matters most
where the genuine operating exposure sits
what should drive client decisions
what does NOT justify overreaction
what would materially change the assessment

Do not simply summarise the report again.


==================================================
13. ANALYST LANGUAGE
==================================================

Remove machine and database language.

DO NOT use:

validated development
validated event
source packet
supplied facts
reporting volume
event count
feed volume
the development is relevant to
the significance depends on whether

where ordinary analytical English works better.

Use language such as:

Current reporting indicates…

The immediate business exposure is…

There is currently no evidence of wider disruption.

The impact remains localised.

The main concern is…

Conditions would worsen if…

The immediate operational effect is…


==================================================
14. REPORT LENGTH AND PAGINATION
==================================================

Do not force every country report to the same length.

Target approximately:

3 to 5 body pages plus cover

depending on the week's intelligence.

Quiet country:

shorter report.

Busy country:

longer report.

Do not pad pages.

Do not create empty pages.

Avoid:

orphan headings
half empty pages
one paragraph occupying an entire page
overcrowded development cards

Content determines pagination.


==================================================

==================================================

Before generating any Country Report confirm:

Cyber collection completed

no feed or database language
