For Fuel reports, include only records about:
- fuel price changes
- fuel shortages
- refinery disruption
- depot disruption
- fuel transport disruption
- fuel protests
- tanker or fuel supply disruption

- general news live blogs

only include records that match the report topic and operational purpose.
