7. WATCH NEXT MUST BE SPECIFIC
==================================================

The current Watch Next contains weak generic wording such as:

"Watch for verified operational follow-through affecting fuel availability, routing or cost."

This is too vague.

Each Watch Next item must identify an observable indicator.

A useful structure is:

LOCATION / THEME

SPECIFIC INDICATOR

WHY IT MATTERS


Examples of the required style:

Further Saudi refinery shutdowns or delayed restart timelines that reduce export product availability.

Verified restrictions or delays affecting fuel cargo transit through the Strait of Hormuz.

Expansion of Russian airport refuelling restrictions to additional airports or carriers.

New rationing or allocation controls affecting commercial diesel or jet-fuel users.


These are style examples only.

Do not hardcode them.
