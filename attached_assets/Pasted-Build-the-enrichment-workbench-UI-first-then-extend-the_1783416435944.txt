Use these source mappings as the starting point:

Corruption — Transparency International CPI
Transparency — Transparency International CPI plus World Bank WGI Voice and Accountability where useful
Regulatory environment — World Bank WGI Regulatory Quality and Rule of Law
Planning and permitting risk — World Bank WGI Regulatory Quality plus analyst review note where no direct permitting data exists
Political stability — World Bank WGI Political Stability
Grid and power stability — World Bank Enterprise Surveys power outage indicators where available
Utility and water supply — World Bank / WRI Aqueduct where available
Water stress — WRI Aqueduct
Subsea cable and connectivity resilience — ITU connectivity indicators as the public baseline, with manual analyst note for subsea-specific gaps
Data localisation and data protection laws — UNCTAD Global Cyberlaw Tracker / DLA Piper Data Protection Laws of the World as source inputs
Land and real estate constraints — public source where available, otherwise not reported
Environmental and climate exposure — ND-GAIN / INFORM where available
Natural hazard exposure — INFORM Risk Index
Security and civil unrest risk — ACLED or existing Workbench incident data where available, otherwise analyst review
Labour and skills availability — World Bank / ILO public indicators where available
Tax, incentives and investment environment — public investment/tax source where available, otherwise not reported

Rules:

Do not fabricate ratings.
Do not write a rating where the source is weak or missing.
Where a source does not exist, mark the dimension as not reported.
Every auto-rating must carry its source and rationale.
Manual analyst override must always be possible.
Analyst-locked fields must not be overwritten by future imports.
The system should generate the first draft automatically, and the analyst should only review, correct or fill gaps.
