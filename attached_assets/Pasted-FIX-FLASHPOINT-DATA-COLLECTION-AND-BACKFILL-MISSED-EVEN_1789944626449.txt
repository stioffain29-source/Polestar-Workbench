DATE CONTROL

For a seven day report:

Only include activity which ACTUALLY OCCURRED within the reporting window.

Distinguish between:

OCCURRED
An event which took place during the reporting period.

ONGOING
An event which began previously but remained active during the reporting period.

PLANNED
A future event which has not yet occurred.

Planned activity must NOT be counted as an occurred incident.

Publication date is not the incident date. Extract and validate the actual event date.

DEDUPLICATION

The same demonstration may appear in numerous news reports.

Do not create multiple incidents for the same event.

Deduplicate using:
country
city
date
organiser
issue
event type

COLLECTION QUALITY CONTROL

Before generating the weekly Flashpoint report, run a coverage check.

Ask:

Have all APAC countries been searched?
Were labour and union sources checked?
Were student demonstrations captured?
Were marches and sit ins captured?
Were road blockades captured?
Were local language or national news sources checked where appropriate?
Were major demonstrations reported by local media but absent from international news feeds captured?
Were events incorrectly classified as general news rather than activism?
Were publication dates confused with incident dates?

If significant events are found during this quality control stage, add them to the incident dataset BEFORE calculating severity, Fast Facts or the Executive Summary.

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Only generate the Executive Summary after collection and quality control are complete.

It must describe the complete regional picture rather than the most recent incident.

It should answer:

What happened across APAC?
Where did meaningful mobilisation occur?
What types of activity were recorded?
Was there violence or significant security force intervention?
What were the practical effects on transport, access, personnel or operations?

Do not produce statements such as:

“The last reported incident was five days ago.”

That logic is meaningless for a multi country regional product.

Do not say:

“Activity clusters in South Korea”

when the database contains significant events in several countries.

The Executive Summary must be generated from the complete seven day APAC dataset, not from whichever country happens to contain the highest severity incident.
