Shipping Watch is not client-ready yet.

Main faults to fix:

1. Related incidents are too noisy
   The report includes speculative or media-framed items as operational incidents, including:

* “Iran Says the Strait of Hormuz Will Stay Closed. What Happens to the US Economy Next?”
* “Iran vows to completely shut Strait of Hormuz as UK mortgage rates face fresh threat”
* “Latest AP News Video…”

These read as commentary, claims or video/news packaging, not confirmed operational shipping incidents.

2. Claims need handling properly
   Iranian statements about closing Hormuz or Bab el-Mandeb should not be treated as confirmed disruption unless there is supporting operational evidence.

Classify these as:

* rhetoric / threat
* advisory watch item
* claim pending confirmation

Do not present them as firm disruption.

3. Country logic is inconsistent
   Regional and country view says the cycle is led by:

* Iran
* Singapore
* Malaysia

But Polestar View says lower volumes are linked to:

* China
* South Korea

That does not align. Fix the country mapping and prose.

4. Chokepoint logic needs tightening
   Hormuz can lead the report, but the report must separate:

* actual vessel attacks
* verified route disruption
* naval/advisory activity
* political threats or claims
* media commentary

Do not let all of these collapse into the same operational category.

5. Latest significant incident is weak
   “Defender-Viper: Royal Navy’s new minehunting drone…” is not a strong latest significant incident for Shipping Watch unless directly tied to current route risk.

Choose a real operational incident, or label it as capability/advisory context.

6. Vessel threat section is better than before
   The attack records are more useful, especially:

* suspected Iranian drone attack on tanker off Oman
* missile likely involved in attack on ship in Strait of Hormuz

Keep this style: specific, operational, maritime-relevant.

7. Commercial impact is thin
   Only one qualifying commercial impact record appears. That is fine, but do not overstate it. Say the commercial signal is limited.

8. Proof required after fix
   Provide:

* revised Shipping Watch PDF
* included records list
* rejected records list with reasons
* confirmation claims/commentary/video items are excluded or labelled correctly
* confirmation country chart and Polestar View use the same dataset
* confirmation Related Incidents contains only operationally relevant records

Do not change branding or layout.

Fix classification, filtering, country consistency and claim handling.
