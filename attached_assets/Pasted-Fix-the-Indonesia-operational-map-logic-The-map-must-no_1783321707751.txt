Fix the Indonesia operational map logic.

The map must not show generic standing risk, assumed congestion, assumed protest exposure, assumed port disruption or assumed crime risk.

The map must only show locations where the current reporting window contains a relevant operational issue.

Core rule:
Do not create a location marker unless there is a specific reported event, issue, disruption, crime incident, protest, fire, regulatory action, transport issue, access issue or other operationally relevant development in that exact area during the reporting period.

Do not infer congestion at Tanjung Priok unless there is reporting of port congestion, port access disruption, labour disruption, gate delay, road disruption or cargo movement impact in the reporting window.

Do not infer CBD disruption unless there is reporting of protest activity, police activity, road closure, access restriction, major event disruption or security incident in the reporting window.

Do not infer crime exposure unless there is a specific crime incident in that location during the reporting window.

One incident does not automatically make an area High.

Assessment logic:
Each mapped location should be assessed using both:

1. Frequency
   How many relevant events occurred in that location or area during the reporting window?

2. Business impact
   What could those events realistically do to business operations, such as movement, site access, staff safety, logistics, utilities, regulatory compliance or continuity?

Severity or posture should be based on frequency plus business impact, not on the presence of a single event.

Use this logic:

Primary operational pinch point:
Multiple relevant reports in the same area, or one event with clear direct business impact.

Secondary operational pinch point:
One or more reports with plausible but limited business impact.

Watch item:
Low frequency reporting with limited current impact, included only because it is operationally relevant.

Do not map:
Generic business districts with no current reporting.
Ports with no reported port issue.
Roads with no reported disruption.
Crime areas with no current crime incident.
Airport routes with no reported airport access issue.
Government districts with no current protest, police or access issue.

Map output requirements:
The map should change week by week based on the reporting, not repeat the same standing locations.

Each marker must answer:
What happened here?
Why does it matter operationally?
What should a business user do about it?

Marker card format:
Location
Reported issue this period
Business relevance
Posture: Primary / Secondary / Watch

Remove:
Generic standing locations.
Unsupported High labels.
Unsupported port congestion.
Unsupported airport corridor warnings.
Unsupported CBD warnings.
Unexplained black markers.
Any wording that implies disruption where there is no current reporting.
