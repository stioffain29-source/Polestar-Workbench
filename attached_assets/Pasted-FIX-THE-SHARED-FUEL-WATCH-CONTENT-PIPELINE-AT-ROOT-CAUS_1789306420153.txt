1. FUEL WATCH MUST ONLY ADMIT FUEL-RELEVANT DEVELOPMENTS
==================================================

Fuel Watch should cover developments with a direct and material relationship to:

fuel production
refining
fuel distribution
fuel shortages
fuel pricing
fuel transport
aviation fuel
marine fuel / bunkering
road fuel
fuel storage
fuel terminals
fuel supply chains
fuel-related strikes / unrest
fuel-related regulatory or subsidy changes
fuel continuity

Do NOT admit an event merely because it concerns:

power
electricity
shipping
war
ports
transport
energy infrastructure

unless there is a clear and evidenced FUEL consequence.


==================================================
2. FIX ENERGY-WATCH LEAKAGE
==================================================

The current report includes generic power-plant shutdown and load-shedding items in WHAT HAPPENED.

Those belong in Energy Watch unless the selected evidence establishes a direct fuel consequence.

Examples of a valid Fuel Watch link:

power failure shuts a fuel terminal
electricity outage interrupts refinery production
load shedding stops fuel pumping or depot operations
generator demand materially tightens diesel availability

Examples that should NOT enter Fuel Watch by themselves:

generic power plant outage
ordinary grid failure
load shedding with no fuel consequence
electricity shortage with no demonstrated effect on fuel supply

Fix this in the shared Fuel relevance/admission logic.

Do not manually remove the two current rows only.


==================================================
3. CROSS-READ EVIDENCE MUST HAVE AN EXPLICIT FUEL LINK
==================================================

Fuel Watch may use bounded cross-read evidence from Shipping, Energy, Conflict or other streams.

But cross-read evidence must pass an explicit Fuel relevance test.

A maritime attack does not automatically become Fuel Watch material.

A conflict incident does not automatically become Fuel Watch material.

A shipping disruption does not automatically become Fuel Watch material.

The accepted cross-read item must establish a credible connection to:

fuel production
fuel availability
fuel transit
fuel prices
fuel distribution
fuel logistics
or fuel continuity

Retain the supporting evidence IDs when cross-read evidence is admitted.


==================================================
4. DO NOT MISATTRIBUTE GLOBAL STORIES TO COUNTRIES
==================================================

The current report turns a global marine-fuel/refining story into a Pakistan country pressure point.

Do not assign a global or regional story to a country merely because:

the source originated there
the article mentions the country
the publication is based there
the article was ingested under that geography

Country attribution must represent the geography of the underlying fuel development.

Before creating:

REGIONAL HIGHLIGHTS
MOST AFFECTED COUNTRY
COUNTRY COUNTS
COUNTRY-SPECIFIC WHAT MATTERS
COUNTRY-SPECIFIC IMPLICATIONS

verify that the underlying development actually occurred in or materially affected that country.


==================================================

==================================================

incident country

regional / global
