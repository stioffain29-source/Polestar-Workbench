3. Tighten Commercial Impact on Shipping filtering

Problem:
Some Commercial Impact records are shipping industry finance or fleet-market items rather than operational shipping impact, for example:
- “Frontline cashes in on ageing suezmax pair”
- “Dynacom lands $65m for ageing suezmax”
- “Ibaizabal heads back to suezmax newbuilds”

These weaken the report. They are shipping market news, not operational impact.

Fix:
Commercial Impact on Shipping should only include items with direct operational or cargo-flow relevance, such as:
- port disruption
- port closure
- strike action affecting shipping/cargo movement
- vessel delay or diversion
- route disruption
- war-risk premium changes
- freight rate movement directly linked to operational conditions
- insurance pressure linked to chokepoint, conflict, piracy, sanctions or vessel security risk
- cargo flow disruption

Exclude from Commercial Impact unless directly linked to operational risk:
- vessel sale and purchase stories
- fleet finance
- newbuild orders
- company earnings
- general shipping market commentary
- pure freight market commentary with no operational shipping linkage
- commercial stories about shipowners unless they affect cargo movement, routing, insurance, port access or vessel security
