COUNTRY LEVEL ENTRIES REQUIRED

Bangladesh:
Government and private offices continue to operate 09:00 to 16:00.
Banking services available 10:00 to 15:00.
Government officials state there is no fuel shortage in the country.
Operational impact: reduced service windows and appointment constraints, but no confirmed national fuel shortage.

India:
Prime Minister Modi urged residents to adopt fuel conservation measures.
Measures include work from home, public transport, reduced cooking oil consumption and limiting foreign travel.
Government has prioritised LPG supply to domestic consumers.
Excise duty cut on petrol and diesel.
Export levies increased on diesel and aviation turbine fuel.
Export duties continue to be adjusted, with the most recent adjustment active as of 16 June 2026.
Industrial fuel supply has been reduced.
Input shortages reported across several factory sectors.
Commercial fuel buyers are banned from purchasing gasoline or diesel from retail stations.
Daily diesel limit of 200 litres per customer or vehicle is in effect.
Air India and IndiGo have reduced domestic and international flight schedules through August.
Routes likely affected include Chicago, Newark, Singapore, Shanghai, San Francisco, Paris and Toronto.
Operational impact: factory disruption, transport constraints, flight disruption and increased business continuity pressure.

Nepal:
Civil service and educational institutions reduced from six day to five day working week.
Government offices operating 09:00 to 17:00, Monday to Friday.
Authorities have urged work from home where possible.
Operational impact: reduced public sector availability and slower administration processing.

Pakistan:
Opening hour restrictions extended until at least 30 June 2026.
Shops, markets and shopping malls can remain open until 21:00.
Standalone groceries and kiryana stores can remain open until 22:00.
Restaurants and food outlets can remain open until 23:00.
Pharmacies, hospitals, fuel stations, IT firms, sports facilities, takeaway and delivery services remain exempt.
Sporadic power outages reported across the country.
Outages are likely to worsen during severe weather or heatwaves.
Operational impact: reduced commercial hours, power reliability issues and disruption to routine operations.

Sri Lanka:
National Fuel Pass System is in place.
Weekly fuel allocation rationing applies by vehicle category.
Operational impact: movement planning required, especially for business travel, essential services and long distance travel.
