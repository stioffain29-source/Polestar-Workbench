I want to scope the technical feasibility of an intelligence production system for a proposed APAC and Oceania ports, terminals and logistics intelligence service.

This is a feasibility and workload assessment. Do not build the full product yet. Do not redesign the existing application or deploy anything.

The proposed service will produce one intelligence bulletin every two weeks.

Geographic coverage:

Southeast Asia
Greater China, including Hong Kong and Taiwan
Japan
South Korea
Australia
New Zealand
Pacific Island states where materially relevant

Excluded:

The Indian Subcontinent

Personnel safety and security

Business continuity
Geopolitical exposure
Regulatory change
Crisis preparedness

FEASIBILITY QUESTIONS

Assess whether the existing Replit application can support this service.

Determine:

1. Which existing feeds, APIs, scrapers and stored sources can already provide relevant material.

2. Which countries and intelligence themes currently have adequate source coverage.

3. Where there are significant source gaps, particularly in local reporting, Pacific Island states, port notices, industrial action, cargo theft and regulatory change.

4. Which suitable sources provide APIs, RSS feeds or other lawful automated access.

5. Which important sources would still require manual review.

6. Whether duplicate reporting of the same incident can be identified and grouped reliably.

7. Whether the system can distinguish routine industry news from events with plausible security, operational or continuity implications.

8. Whether automated geographic and thematic exclusions can reliably remove Subcontinent, Middle East and Red Sea reporting.

9. Whether the system can identify the port, terminal, city, transport corridor and affected country from collected reporting.

10. Whether the system can distinguish confirmed facts, unverified claims and analytical judgement.

11. Whether it can generate structured draft intelligence items while retaining the original source and publication date.

12. How much human review would still be required for verification, selection, assessment and final editing.

13. Whether the system can export the selected material into an editable Microsoft Word format.

14. What would be required to add a client specific watchlist of ports, terminals, markets and corridors.

15. What technical, legal, source reliability or continuity risks could prevent dependable fortnightly production.
