Sub project: Jakarta Operational Map Trial

Objective

Create a trial version of the Jakarta city report map.

This must be a separate test component, page, or report section. Do not replace the existing live PDF map yet.

The aim is to move from an incident dot map to an operational exposure map.

The map should answer:

Where are the operating pressure points?
What is the exposure level?
What should the client do before movement?

Current problem

The current Jakarta map plots too many incidents and becomes cluttered around Central Jakarta.

It shows data, but it does not clearly show judgement.

The client should not have to decode every marker. They need a clear operating picture.

Required approach

Build the map around operating zones, not individual incidents.

Use these Jakarta operating zones:

1. Central Jakarta government district
2. Tanjung Priok port
3. North Jakarta access roads
4. Sudirman and Thamrin business corridor
5. SCBD and Senayan
6. Kuningan and Gatot Subroto
7. Soekarno Hatta airport corridor

Each zone needs:

Zone number
Zone name
Exposure rating
Short reason
Recommended movement action

Exposure ratings:

High
Elevated
Monitored
Low
Not assessed

Map requirements

Show numbered operating zones on the map.

Show clear route corridors for:

Airport corridor
Port corridor
CBD business corridor
North Jakarta access

Use simple exposure shading or outlines.

Use individual incident markers only where they add operational value.

Do not show every incident.

Reduce clutter in the CBD.

The right hand panel must match the numbered zones on the map.

Right hand panel title

Movement posture this period

Example right hand panel format:

1. Central Jakarta government district
Rating: High
Reason: Protest exposure around government and civic areas.
Action: Confirm activity around Monas, Istana Merdeka and DPR/MPR before non essential movement.

2. Tanjung Priok port
Rating: High
Reason: Port access and container movement exposure.
Action: Confirm terminal and gate status before dispatch.

3. North Jakarta access roads
Rating: High
Reason: Low lying access roads and flooding exposure.
Action: Check Cilincing and Koja approaches before movement.

4. Sudirman and Thamrin business corridor
Rating: Elevated
Reason: Business and hotel corridor with congestion and protest spillover exposure.
Action: Confirm road status before client meetings and executive movement.

5. SCBD and Senayan
Rating: Elevated
Reason: Office, hotel and venue cluster.
Action: Confirm SCBD and Senayan approaches before meetings and events.

6. Kuningan and Gatot Subroto
Rating: Elevated
Reason: Embassy and office corridor.
Action: Confirm Gatot Subroto and Rasuna Said status before movement.

7. Soekarno Hatta airport corridor
Rating: Monitored
Reason: City to airport transfer route.
Action: Confirm airport toll road and Tangerang approach before departure.

Legend requirements

Keep the legend simple.

It should show:

High
Elevated
Monitored
Low
Not assessed
Operating zone
Movement corridor
Major incident marker

Methodology note

Move long methodology notes away from the main map area.

Use this short note only on the map page:

Map reflects assessed operating exposure for this reporting period. Incident points are shown only where location detail is sufficient and operationally useful.

Trial output required

Produce one test version of the Jakarta operational map section with:

Clean operational map
Numbered operating zones
Movement posture panel
Clear exposure ratings
Simple legend
Short methodology note
PDF safe layout

The output must be suitable for insertion into the existing Jakarta city report PDF.

Acceptance criteria

The trial is successful if:

The CBD is readable without clutter.
The zone numbers are easy to match with the right hand panel.
The client can understand movement posture in under 30 seconds.
The map shows judgement, not just raw incident data.
The design looks like a professional risk product.
The existing live report system is not broken.
The old map remains available for comparison.

Important constraints

This is a trial sub project only.

Do not refactor the full Workbench.
Do not change unrelated report sections.
Do not alter the existing PDF export flow unless needed for a trial copy.
Do not remove the current map.
Do not push this into the live report until reviewed and approved.
