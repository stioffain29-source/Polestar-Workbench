WATCH_NEXT_UNGROUNDED should be a non-fatal validation warning.

Watch Next is inherently forward-looking. It is not appropriate to treat every questionable forward indicator as a fatal consistency failure.

3. ONLY GENUINELY SERIOUS VALIDATION FAILURES SHOULD BLOCK FINAL EXPORT

Hard blocking should be reserved for issues such as:

direct contradiction of validated facts
unsupported casualty figures
unsupported locations
invented incidents
materially incorrect risk ratings
impossible or contradictory dates
report content referring to evidence that does not exist

Do NOT hard-block because of:

Watch Next grounding uncertainty
wording differences
semantic paraphrasing
non-critical analytical judgement differences

Implement at least:

ERROR
Serious factual or structural failure. May block final export.

WARNING

INFO
Quality or editorial suggestion only.
