Fix the Indonesia report Operational Map impact logic and wording.

Problem:
The map cards are currently over-classifying incidents as Direct impact and using inflated language such as "evacuate" where the correct meaning is simply "moved", "removed", "escorted", "relocated", or "cleared".

Impact logic must be tightened:

Direct impact:
Use only when the reporting shows a current or confirmed effect on client operations, movement, access, site security, utilities, production, logistics, workforce safety, or business continuity.

Indirect impact:
Use when the incident is relevant to the operating environment but does not directly affect a client site, route, workforce, movement, production, access, utilities, or logistics.

Monitor only:
Use for background incidents, preparedness meetings, isolated local crime, political/legal developments, corruption investigations, or general policing activity unless there is a clear operational effect.

Specific rule:
Do not mark an incident Direct impact merely because it happened in a region where clients may operate. There must be a stated operational consequence.

Language correction:
- Do not use "evacuated" unless people were formally evacuated from danger.
- For police/security movement of a suspect or crowd control, use:
  "moved"
  "escorted"
  "removed from the area"
  "cleared from the scene"
- Avoid alarmist language.

Examples from current Indonesia map:
- Cilegon city government flood preparedness meeting = Monitor only or Indirect, not Direct.
- Semarang warehouse fire = Indirect unless it is a client warehouse or causes route/access/logistics disruption.
- Sumatra power corruption / outage reporting = Indirect unless a current outage is confirmed affecting operations.
- Kalimantan coal corruption / power outage reporting = Indirect unless a current operational outage is confirmed.
- Kendari market fire = Indirect unless client site, route, staff, or logistics affected.
- Mataram donation box theft suspect moved by police = Monitor only. Wording must not say evacuated.
