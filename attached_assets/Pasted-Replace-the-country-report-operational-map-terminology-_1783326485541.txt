Replace the country report operational map terminology.

Remove the terms:
- Posture
- Primary operational pinch point
- Secondary operational pinch point
- Watch item

These terms are unclear and sound like invented internal language.

Use plain business language instead:

1. Direct impact
Use only where current reporting shows an issue that can realistically affect business operations now.
Examples:
- road closure
- protest blocking access
- fire affecting an industrial area
- port access disruption
- airport access disruption
- security incident near a site or route
- utility outage affecting operations

2. Possible impact
Use where current reporting is relevant to business operations but the impact is limited, indirect or not yet confirmed.
Examples:
- protest planned near a business district
- crime incident near a movement route
- local disruption that may affect access
- regulatory action that may affect a site or sector

3. Monitor only
Use where reporting is relevant but there is no clear current business disruption.
Examples:
- isolated low impact incident
- early warning report
- issue outside main operating areas
- situation that could develop but has not yet affected movement, access or operations

Core rule:
Only map locations where the current reporting window contains a specific operationally relevant issue.

Do not map places just because they are generally important.

Do not infer:
- port congestion unless there is current reporting of port congestion or port access disruption
- airport disruption unless there is current reporting affecting the airport or airport access
- CBD disruption unless there is current protest, police, road closure or access reporting
- crime exposure unless there is a specific crime incident in that location
- government district exposure unless there is current protest, policing or access reporting

One incident does not automatically create Direct impact.
The label must reflect both:
- frequency of reporting
- likely business impact

Marker card format:
Location
What happened this period
Business relevance
Impact level: Direct impact / Possible impact / Monitor only

Map title:
Operational Map

Map subtitle:
Reported operational issues this period

Map note:
This map shows reported operational issues for the current reporting period. Locations are included only where reporting indicates a relevant event, issue or disruption. Impact level is based on likely business impact and reporting frequency, not standing background risk.

Acceptance criteria:
- No use of posture, primary, secondary or watch terminology.
- Every map marker must be tied to a current reporting item.
- No generic standing locations.
- No unsupported port, airport, CBD or road warnings.
- One isolated low impact incident must not be labelled Direct impact unless the business effect is clear.
- Map labels, cards and narrative must use the same terminology.
- Preview and PDF export must match.
