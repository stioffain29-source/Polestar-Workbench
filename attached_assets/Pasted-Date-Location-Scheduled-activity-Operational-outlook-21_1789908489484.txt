Date	Location	Scheduled activity	Operational outlook
21 Sep	Manila, Philippines	Multiple demonstrations marking the 54th anniversary of Martial Law, alongside sectoral activity and a transport strike. Police are monitoring Mendiola, Luneta, Liwasang Bonifacio, Ayala Bridge, People Power Monument and EDSA Shrine. Road closures begin from 00:01. Around 15,000 police personnel are being deployed.	High confidence. Significant congestion and public transport disruption are likely in central Manila. Large gatherings are expected, although current reporting does not indicate a specific threat of serious violence.
21 Sep onward	Islamabad, Pakistan	Jamaat e Islami launched a march towards Islamabad on 20 September over the fuel levy. Government talks had not resolved the dispute as of 20 September and authorities were strengthening security around the capital.	Carryover watch. Potential for roadblocks, checkpoints and disruption around approaches to Islamabad early in the week.
24 Sep	Jakarta, Indonesia	Several protest themes converge around National Farmers Day, the Semanggi II anniversary and the wider “Black September” mobilisation. Indonesian police have publicly identified 24 September as the expected peak of demonstrations.	High confidence. Central Jakarta should be treated as a significant public order and traffic disruption environment.
24 Sep	Jakarta, Indonesia	KSPSI has separately announced a major labour demonstration if parliament does not provide a satisfactory response on the Employment Bill by 22 September. Organisers have stated an intention to mobilise up to 50,000 motorcycles from Tangerang, Bekasi, Karawang and Purwakarta, concentrating around MH Thamrin, Grand Indonesia and Bundaran HI.	Conditional but significant. Confirmation should be checked after the 22 September deadline. If it proceeds at the announced scale, expect substantial traffic disruption across Jakarta approaches as well as the CBD.
27 Sep	Islamabad and nationwide, Pakistan	PTI has called a nationwide protest and long march to Islamabad demanding the release of Imran Khan and other political demands. Party officials say demonstrations are intended in cities and districts nationwide. Authorities have begun preventive measures, including the arrest of Imran Khan's sister in Lahore on 20 September, while the Interior Minister has said the government intends to prevent the Islamabad march.
