Port Moresby / NCD

10 June 2026, reported. Incident overnight 9–10 June.
Correctional Services firearms / disorder incident. Police detained 20 Correctional Services personnel after a disorderly incident involving alleged illegal firearm discharge, assault and threats. RPNGC said it began with a confrontation at a CS barracks and moved to a residence in NCD, where arrests were made. Vehicles and weapons were impounded as evidence.

10 June 2026, reported by PNG Haus Bung. Incident began at Kerepia Barracks, Bomana, and ended around Gerehu / Rainbow area.
Same CS incident, with local reporting saying officers were detained in Port Moresby after an internal Correctional Services dispute linked to the acting commissioner appointment. PNG Haus Bung reported the group was allegedly armed and intoxicated.

9 June 2026, reported.
Nine Mile / Bomana community policing launch. Not an incident, but relevant as crime mitigation. RPNGC said Nine Mile Police Station launched community policing activity to work with schools, markets and community leaders in the Nine Mile and Bomana areas.

Lae

9 June 2026, reported / updated 18 June. Actual operation 26 May 2026.
West Taraka police raid. Morobe police and the Lae-based Sector Response Unit conducted a raid targeting a wanted criminal linked to armed hold-up, manslaughter and drug-related activity. The suspect was secured and taken to Angau General Hospital under police presence. Two accomplices were also apprehended.

9 June 2026, reported.
Lae police intelligence training completed. Not a crime incident, but relevant to security posture. RPNGC said officers from Morobe, Madang, Eastern Highlands and Lae Metropolitan Command completed intelligence training in Lae.

Mt Hagen

12 June 2026, reported. Works began 4 June.
Kagamuga Airport runway works underway. Not protest or crime, but relevant for movement. PNG Haus Bung reported runway repairs at Mt Hagen’s Kagamuga Airport started on 4 June and are expected to take about nine months, weather permitting.

No fresh protest, theft, robbery or major crime incident found in open reporting for Mt Hagen during 12–19 June. The only current Mt Hagen item I found was the airport works. Older reporting from May flagged police action around the city market for bag snatching, armed robbery and petty crime, but I would not present that as “this week”.
