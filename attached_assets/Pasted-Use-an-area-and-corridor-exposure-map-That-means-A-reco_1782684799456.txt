Use an area and corridor exposure map.

That means:

A recognisable outline of Jakarta and the surrounding Greater Jakarta area
Clear district or zone boundaries
Shaded or outlined exposure areas
Key movement corridors shown as route bands or arrows
Short labels or callouts explaining why each area matters

WHAT THE MAP MUST SHOW

The map should show these business relevant areas:

Central Jakarta government district
Protest and policing disruption
Main commercial and hotel areas
Office, client meeting and visitor exposure
Soekarno Hatta airport corridor
Airport transfer disruption
North Jakarta and port area
Logistics, flooding and access exposure
Greater Jakarta commuter belt
Rain, congestion and staff commute exposure
Main cross city movement routes
Delays affecting meetings, site visits and deliveries

HOW TO SHOW IT

Use a proper map base, then overlay:

Shaded polygons or outlined zones for exposure areas
Route bands or arrows for airport corridor and cross city movement
Short callout labels for each area
Simple legend showing status such as monitored, elevated or high

PREFERRED VISUAL APPROACH

This should look like a clean report map.

Use:

pale grey base map
thin district boundaries
Polestar colours for exposure overlays
clear labels
no clutter

Do not use:

raw incident dots
tiny numbered markers as the main method
abstract dashed lines
decorative icons floating in space
cloud icons
web map controls
map attribution clutter
a diagram that stops looking like Jakarta

LABEL STYLE

Each area should be labelled directly on or next to the map, for example:

Central Jakarta government district
Protest and policing disruption

North Jakarta and port
Logistics, flooding and access exposure

Soekarno Hatta airport corridor
Airport transfer disruption

Greater Jakarta commuter belt
Rain, congestion and staff commute exposure

COMMERCIAL AREAS

If possible, identify the main commercial/business zone clearly rather than using a vague label. If South Jakarta is the main office and embassy concentration, show that. If the commercial core spans more than one district, label it accordingly.

WHAT THE MAP IS FOR

The map must answer this question:

Where in Jakarta could movement, access, logistics or business activity be disrupted this week?

If the map does not answer that clearly, it is not the right map.
