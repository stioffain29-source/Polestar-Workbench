Suggested Polestar View angle:

Data centre development is increasingly shaped by power availability, water access, planning approval, land use politics, environmental scrutiny and local community tolerance. For operators, investors, insurers and suppliers, the risk is no longer just whether a facility can be built, but whether the surrounding infrastructure, regulation and social licence can support it.
