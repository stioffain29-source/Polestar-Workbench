Build this as a PNG Country Report ingest and reporting module, not as separate city feeds.

The PNG Country Report should monitor the last 7 days of open-source reporting for protest, civil unrest, crime, theft, robbery, tribal violence, policing operations and operational disruption.

Core monitored areas:
- Port Moresby / NCD
- Lae / Morobe
- Mt Hagen / Western Highlands
- National catch-all for major incidents elsewhere in PNG that affect security, travel, aviation, ports, roads, fuel, power, telecoms or government stability.

Sources:
- RPNGC
- Post Courier
- The National PNG
- PNG Haus Bung
- Loop PNG
- EMTV
- One PNG
- ReliefWeb
- GDELT
- official government, airport and port statements where available

Do not rely on police reporting alone. Treat RPNGC as high-confidence confirmation, not the only discovery source.

For each article or item, extract:
- source
- title
- URL
- published date
- incident date if mentioned
- location
- province
- city or area
- incident category
- short factual summary
- business impact
- confidence score
- whether the incident occurred this reporting week
- whether it was only reported this week but occurred earlier
- reason included or rejected

The report should produce:
1. Executive summary
2. Top 3 incidents this week
3. Port Moresby / NCD section
4. Lae / Morobe section
5. Mt Hagen / Western Highlands section
6. Other national security-relevant incidents
7. Business impact
8. Outlook for next week
9. Source confidence and reporting gaps

If no reportable item is found for a location, write:
“No fresh publicly reported protest, theft, robbery or major crime incident identified in open sources for this location during the reporting period.”

Add ingest diagnostics showing:
- articles pulled by source
- articles matching PNG
- articles matching monitored locations
- articles matching incident terms
- rejected duplicates
- rejected old incidents
- rejected non-security items
- promoted report candidates

The aim is a usable PNG country security report, not a raw news feed.
