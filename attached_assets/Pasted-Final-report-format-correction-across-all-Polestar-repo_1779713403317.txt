Final report-format correction across all Polestar reports.

Apply to:
- Fuel Watch
- Shipping Watch
- Cargo Watch
- Flashpoint
- Energy Watch
- Fertiliser Watch
- any other report using the same PDF/report template

Do not change:
- data plumbing
- classification logic
- Fast Facts
- charts
- colours
- fonts
- cover design
- report titles

Required changes:

1. Remove Source Notes / Data Notes completely

Delete the Source Notes / Data Notes section from all report PDFs and previews.

Do not show:
- SOURCE NOTES / DATA NOTES
- “Based on records held in the Polestar Workbench...”
- “Records without coordinates...”
- “Severity ratings follow...”
- exclusion counts
- dedupe notes
- internal methodology notes
- “records were excluded...”
- “syndicated duplicate was collapsed...”

These are internal workbench notes and should not appear in client-facing reports.

Keep only:
- Disclaimer

2. Business Implications must be short bullet points

The Flashpoint Implications section is too long and reads like a wall of text.

Convert Implications for Business into short, practical bullet points across all reports.

Format:
- 4 to 7 bullets maximum
- one line each where possible
- direct operational action
- no long paragraphs

Example for Flashpoint:

Implications for Business
- Review staff movement plans for cities with active protest calls.
- Set clear work-from-home or delayed-start triggers for affected locations.
- Confirm alternate routes for staff, visitors and delivery movements.
- Check site access controls for offices, plants and public-facing facilities.
- Pre-approve customer and staff communications for disruption days.
- Monitor police restrictions, Section 144 orders, arrests and road closures.
- Align security, logistics and HR on escalation thresholds.

3. Watch Next should be concise

Watch Next should not become a long essay.

Use short bullets.

For Flashpoint:
- If there are actual future-dated protest / strike / mobilisation items, list them clearly.
- If no future-dated items exist, state that clearly and then list watch indicators.

Format:
- 5 to 8 bullets maximum
- each bullet starts with the signal
- then one short operational meaning

Example:
- PTI mobilisation in Pakistan: watch for road closures near courts, party offices and administrative districts.
- Indian chemists’ strike activity: monitor pharmacy closures and local service disruption.
- South Korea labour action: watch for union escalation around Samsung-linked activity.
- Section 144 / police restrictions: trigger journey-management review.
- Arrests or injuries: likely to harden protest response and extend disruption.

Do not write long paragraphs here.

4. Reduce white space

The Flashpoint PDF has ridiculous white space across pages.

Fix pagination and spacing:
- reduce oversized top gaps after page breaks
- do not push sections halfway down a page
- keep section heading and first content together
- avoid large blank areas before Watch Next / Polestar View
- remove Source Notes to recover space
- make the report compact but readable

5. Keep Disclaimer only

At the end of each report:
- show Disclaimer
- no Source Notes
- no Data Notes
- no internal methodology

If the disclaimer can fit on the previous page, pull it back.
Do not leave a dead final page with only a short disclaimer unless unavoidable.

Export fresh PDFs for:

- Flashpoint

Confirm:
- no Source Notes / Data Notes section appears anywhere
- Implications for Business is bullet-pointed
- Watch Next is concise and operational
