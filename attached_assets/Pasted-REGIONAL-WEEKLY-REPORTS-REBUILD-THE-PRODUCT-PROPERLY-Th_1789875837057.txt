4. DEDUPLICATION
==================================================

ONE REAL-WORLD EVENT = ONE DEVELOPMENT.

Multiple articles about the same event are corroborating sources.

They are not separate developments.

Do not allow a country to dominate because several outlets covered one incident.

Source count may increase confidence.

It must not increase materiality.

7. SEVERITY
==================================================

Use only:

INSIGNIFICANT
LOW
MODERATE
HIGH
EXTREME

Severity means CURRENT CONSEQUENCE.

It does NOT mean:

media interest
regional importance
article volume
dramatic headline
political significance

INSIGNIFICANT

Negligible consequence.

LOW

Limited local inconvenience or minor consequence.

MODERATE

Credible operational impact requiring adjustment.

HIGH

Significant violence, disruption or restriction with material consequences for people, operations, travel, assets, logistics or continuity.

EXTREME

Major or widespread consequences, major conflict escalation, mass casualty event, severe infrastructure disruption or sustained large-scale effect.

Security events must consider:

attack method
target
fatalities
injuries
intent
location
follow-on threat
security response
access effect
operational consequence

Regulatory events must consider:

number affected
implementation date
workforce effect
compliance burden
travel effect
market-access effect
actual operational disruption

A visa-policy change must NOT receive the same severity treatment as a mass-casualty bombing merely because both matter to business.

Regional significance and severity are separate judgements.
