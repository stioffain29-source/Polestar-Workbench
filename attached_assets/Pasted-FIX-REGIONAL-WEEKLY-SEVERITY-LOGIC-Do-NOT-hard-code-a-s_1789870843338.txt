SEVERITY DEFINITIONS

INSIGNIFICANT
Negligible operational consequence.
No meaningful effect on people, access, operations or continuity.

LOW
Limited inconvenience or localised effect.
Little operational adjustment required.

MODERATE
Credible disruption or threat requiring operational adjustment.
May affect movement, access, staffing, compliance or services in a defined area.

HIGH
Significant violence, disruption or restriction with material consequences for:
people
operations
travel
assets
transport
supply chains
continuity

EXTREME
Major or widespread consequences involving:
mass casualties
major conflict escalation
severe infrastructure disruption
sustained large-scale operational impact

FOR SECURITY INCIDENTS assess:

intent
weapon / attack method
target
fatalities
injuries
location
follow-on threat
security response
access restrictions
effect on commercial movement
potential operational consequence

DO NOT reduce severity simply because an incident is geographically localised.

A LOCAL bombing can still be HIGH.

Regional significance and incident severity are TWO DIFFERENT THINGS.

FOR REGULATORY ITEMS assess:

scale of affected population
effective date
business compliance burden
workforce impact
travel impact
market-access impact
whether operations are actually disrupted

Administrative inconvenience must not automatically become HIGH.
