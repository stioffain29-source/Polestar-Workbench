Map Content

2. Main commercial and hotel areas
   Use an amber translucent zone around the main commercial / hotel area.

3. North Jakarta port and logistics area
   Use an amber translucent zone around Tanjung Priok / North Jakarta logistics area.

4. Soekarno Hatta airport corridor
   Use a translucent corridor from the airport towards central Jakarta.

5. Cross city movement routes
   Use soft translucent route bands across the city, not thin random lines.

6. Greater Jakarta commuter belt
   Use a subtle dashed boundary or wide translucent context zone around the wider commuting area.

Overlay Style

Use soft transparent overlays, around 20 to 35 percent opacity.

Use rounded corridor bands rather than hard angular lines.

Use restrained colours:

High: red
Elevated: amber / orange
Monitored: muted yellow
Low: muted green
Not assessed / context: light grey

The overlays should sit lightly on the map and allow the base map to remain readable.
